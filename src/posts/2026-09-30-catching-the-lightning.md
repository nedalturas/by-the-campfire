A stroke of luck opened before me.\
It seemed I had qualified for it unknowingly....

I have known myself to be timid. I saw the door,\
I shied away from it...

Regret would render me restless.\
Consuming the soul that wants to go...

Against the current, I am often swept away. I let myself be-\
without will to fight it, doubting what I could carry.

Until I put down a wager, \
and give myself the odds  even on the slimmest chance.\
\
A shot in the dark...

I tried, \
knowing I would more often lose than win\
\
And I lost the battle\
but won the war.

Because the attrition \
was never about how I failed to emerge victorious....\
\
it was how I rise with a trembling knees \
and stand before Goliath.

T'was about pushing the stone uphill...\
Only to watch it roll back down.\
\
T'was about clawing my way out of quicksand,\
with whatever strength remained.\
\
And when I stood at the edge of surrender,\
arms heavy,\
strength leaving me, slowly losing my breath...\
\
I asked.

The heavens, this time...\
Please give me not a lighter weight, but a broader shoulders...\
\
Give me the strength,\
To stubbornly make appearance to the devils within me...

again and again,\
as the Spartans did.

Yes, \
I may tear my tendons, \
like Achilles.\
\
And I may walk with a limp.\
\
But by God's grace, call me...\
then I'll catch that lightning. \
\
when Zeus strikes,\
I'll climb Olympus Mons, ceaseless...\
\
Scathed, shattered\
bloodied, with sweat...\
\
I'll stand.\
\
I win.
