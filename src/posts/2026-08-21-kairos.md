I don’t believe in meeting someone at the wrong time.

\
I never understood the idea\
that two souls can find each other\
at the wrong moment,\
as if time itself was cruel enough\
to place two people together\
only to pull them apart.

Perhaps we call it the wrong time\
because it is easier to blame the clock\
than to accept the choices we made.

\
But I believe every person we meet\
arrives exactly when they are supposed to.

\
Not a day too early.\
Not a day too late.\
Not by accident.\
Every meeting carries a reason\
we may not understand at first.\
\
Some people arrive to stay.\
Some arrive to teach us.\
Some arrive to change the way\
we look at love, at ourselves,\
and at the life we thought we wanted.

\
And some arrive\
only to remind us\
that our hearts are still capable\
of feeling something real.

\
So no,\
I will never call what happened between us\
the wrong time.

\
We met.\
\
And that alone means something.

\
Out of all the people in this world,\
out of all the places we could have been,\
all the days we could have lived,\
all the decisions that could have been different\
somehow, our paths crossed.

\
How could I call that wrong?

\
Maybe we were simply two people\
standing at the same door,\
but choosing different ways to walk through it.

\
Maybe we wanted the same destination\
but were not willing to take\
the same road.

\
And that is not the fault of time.

\
Because time does not choose for us.

\
We do.

\
We choose whether to stay.\
We choose whether to fight.\
We choose whether to understand.\
We choose whether to forgive.\
We choose whether to reach for someone's hand\
or slowly let it slip away.

\
Every choice creates another road,\
and every road eventually becomes\
the life we live.

\
So when something between two people\
doesn't work,\
I don't want to say,



"Maybe we met at the wrong time."

\
Maybe we met at exactly the right time,\
but we were given the freedom\
to choose what we would do with it.

\
Maybe the universe didn't promise\
that meeting someone meant keeping them.

\
Maybe it only promised\
that when they arrived,\
we would have the chance\
to choose.

\
To choose love.\
To choose courage.\
To choose each other.

\
And perhaps that is the most beautiful\
and painful part of being human

\
that sometimes,\
the right person can stand before us\
and still,\
we can choose another path.

\
Not because they were wrong.

\
Not because the timing was wrong.

\
But because we are human,\
and our choices belong to us.

\
I believe everything we experience\
is connected by invisible threads.

\
The conversations we almost didn't have.\
The places we almost didn't go.\
The messages we almost didn't send.\
The nights we almost stayed home.\
The moment we decided to say yes\
instead of no.

\
All of them lead us somewhere.

\
And somehow,\
all those small choices\
led me to you.

\
So I refuse to call you\
a wrong-time person.

\
You were never a mistake\
in the calendar of my life.

\
You were a chapter\
that existed because it was meant to exist.

\
And whatever happened between us\
the laughter, the silence,\
the closeness, the distance,\
the promises we kept\
and the ones we couldn't\
I believe they all belonged\
to the story we were writing.

\
Perhaps our story was never meant\
to be about forever.

\
Perhaps it was meant to be about\
the moment when two people\
found each other\
and realized they could choose love.

\
And if choosing me\
means choosing you,

\
then I would choose you.\
\
Not because fate forced my hand,\
but because my heart\
made its own decision.

\
And if choosing you\
means choosing me,

\
then perhaps there is no wrong time\
at all.

\
Perhaps there is only\
the courage to choose\
and the courage to accept\
what our choices create.

\
Because love, to me,\
has never been about perfect timing.

\
It has always been about intention.

\
About two people looking at each other\
and saying,

\
"Of all the paths I could take,\
I choose the one that leads to you."

\
And if someday\
we find ourselves standing\
at another crossroads,

\
I hope we don't blame the clock.

\
I hope we don't say\
we loved each other at the wrong time.

\
I hope we are honest enough to say,

\
"We were given a choice,\
and this is the path we chose."

\
Because there is something sacred\
about taking responsibility\
for the direction of your own life.

\
We cannot blame the stars\
for the roads we refused to take.

\
We cannot blame time\
for the decisions we made.

\
And we cannot call something wrong\
simply because it didn't become\
what we wanted it to become.



Some things can be right

even when they end.



Some people can be right

even when they don't stay.



Some love can be real

even when it doesn't become forever.



And some meetings

can be beautiful

without needing to last a lifetime.



So if you ever ask me

whether we met at the wrong time,



my answer will always be no.



We met at the only time

we could have met



the time when our paths

were meant to cross.



What happened after that

was ours to choose.



And perhaps that is what makes

our story both beautiful and ugly at the same time.



We were not victims of time.



We were simply two people

given the same moment,

the same possibility,

the same chances to choose



and standing there,

with the whole future waiting ahead of us,



we chose our paths.



If one day

those paths lead us back to each other,

I will not call it fate correcting a mistake.



I will call it

two people choosing each other again.



And if they never do,



I will still be grateful.



Because I would rather believe

that meeting you was right

even if losing you hurt,



than spend my life believing

that something beautiful

was somehow wrong

just because it didn't last.



You were not the wrong person.



We were not the wrong time.



We were simply two souls

who met at the exact point

where our lives were meant to touch.



And for however long

our roads walked beside each other, 



I will always believe

that meeting you

was one of the nicest things,

life ever gave me.
