Life is a boat upon the sea,

where drifting waves calm my mind.

Yet you are the noise of the forest,

where I am the bird flying home

in every presence of you.

I admit that it is foolhardy,

but I love how it feels.

Watching you feels like a portrait captured by a camera,

where everything is blurry...

excluding you.

It’s the most beautiful smile—hollow, yet enough to disarm my heart.

Knowing this, I still give you my true love, reckless of the cost.

Maybe I forgot how love feels.

In my heart, I know

you are not constant like the Northern Star—

only another passing constellation.

Yes, I will let you use me,

just as you have done for everyone you’ve met.

I don't—and will not—regret it,

and I am not finished yet...

falling into...

your...

Fool's Gold.
