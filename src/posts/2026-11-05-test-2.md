Meeting you was an inevitable destiny.\
Woven intricately on the Fates' ancient loom.\
Her voice, a gentle hymn even sorrow envies,\
could soothe even the underworld's rampaging flames.\
\
Your arrival is a blessing, so is your every smile.\
They glimmer so radiantly even Astraeus' stars envy.\
How you shine against the vast and hollow night.\
\
No amount of gold could ever hold them,\
the amber glowing in your eyes like the Sun eternalized.\
\
Yet how fickle destiny can be,\
the evil string slithered — lives sapped by the venom.\
Since then, no melody could deafen the silence,\
then sound of a grieving heart no longer beating.\
\
Your absence feels colder than a thousand winters,\
emptier than the barren fields in drought.\
\
Oh, how could a love so fierce simply end in dust?\
Traversing the underworld to grant a second trust.\
To walk the ash-filled path of Hades' domain, \
to chase for a glimpse of your light again.\
\
And at the very edge of Eos' wake...\
how could I not look back?\
\
For temptation is a shadow no mortal can outrun,\
and doubts devoured the remains of my belief in gods.\
\
Then I saw her, holier than sacred. Grander than any divine.\
\
So I turned, and in hell I kissed the Sun once anew,\
finding in your eyes of amber a reflection of my soul.\
My heart has yet to beat again, and you slowly vanish.\
How could it be, was our love always meant for anguish?\
\
Not anguish, then, but the price of having known you.\
Should the gods have jested, and their promise be a lie,\
to grasp your light that's sweeter than morning dew,\
was worth the ash, the dark, and this goodbye.\
\
For no thread the Fates spun, no shears of Atropos’ hand,\
could sever what rhymes your soul with mine.\
Let the loom unravel, let Olympus disband,\
my soul, through a thousand lifetimes, keeps your shrine.
