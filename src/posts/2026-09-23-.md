> "Everything we hear is an opinion, not a fact. Everything we see is a perspective, not the truth."\
> — Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations*

We only perceive what we allow ourselves to grasp. Every action is built upon preconceived thought notions and socially accepted truths embedded deep within humanity. Though it makes me wonder, are we merely fools under the guise of an intellectual? \
\
We walk through this world convinced we are looking through a clear glass window, when in truth we are looking into a mirror dirtied with the dust of everything that we think we know. Rarely does it shatter, though, as people would rather look at their distorted and deceptive reflection than to let their belief fractured into fragments of truth they don't want to swallow.\
\
We pride ourselves as the apex predator, standing on top amongst creatures with fangs and claws, due to our capacity to think. An animal acts on instincts and habits to survive, it does not construct elaborate systems of denial to protect its fragile ego. The fortress-walls that we call society putrefying the curious intelligence of mankind, blanketing the mind with comfortable lies. We have reached planets and stars, observed the cosmos at large, yet remain shackled with societal standards.\
\
