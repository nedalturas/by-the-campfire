> "The only true wisdom is in knowing you know nothing.."\
> — *Socrates*

We only perceive what we allow ourselves to grasp. Every action is built upon preconceived thought notions and socially accepted truths embedded deep within humanity. Though it makes me wonder, are we merely fools under the guise of an intellectual? \
\
We walk through this world convinced we are looking through a clear glass window, when in truth we are looking into a mirror dirtied with the dust of everything that we think we know. Rarely does it shatter, though, as people would rather look at their distorted and deceptive reflection than to let their belief fractured into fragments of truth they don't want to swallow.\
\
We pride ourselves as the apex predator, standing on top amongst creatures with fangs and claws, due to our capacity to think. Animals act on instincts and habits to survive, they do not construct elaborate systems of denial to protect its fragile ego. The fortress walls that we call society, putrefying the curious intelligence of mankind, blanketing the mind with comfortable lies. We have reached planets and stars, observed the cosmos, yet remain largely shackled with societal standards.\
\
We thus have fallen into a deeper trap than ferity ever could: we have the hubris of assuming intelligence is wisdom, and that assumption is what let curiosity fade. Yet curiosity, once smothered, does not simply vanish, it leaves a vacancy that became complacency. It does not arrive as an enemy, it arrives as comfort, as convenience, as the relief of no longer having to ask. Society, and those who profit from our stillness, do not need to conquer a mind that has already stopped defending itself.\
\
With the truth twisted by cruel thinkers acting as leaders, enlighteners to a greater tomorrow. We are but mere sheep fattened up not for our nourishment, but for the harvest of their gains. Institutions do not need to chain a mind it has already convinced to stay. They need only mandate the pasture: what to see, what to follow, what is trending, what is true this week and forgotten the next.\
\
Every mandate arrives wrapped in the language of the collective good, rarely does it announce itself as what it often is. This is the genius of the modern leash, as it does not demand obedience, it manufactures consent. A ruler who must force compliance has already failed, but a ruler who is thanked for the chains has succeeded completely.\
\
Recency dressed as relevance, popularity dressed as truth, and the herd moves as one, not because it was forced, but because it was taught to follow the tide.\
\
But a mirror, no matter how dirtied, can still be wiped clean by the hands willing to reach for it. The wooly blanket of comfort is warm and heavy, as it is meant to keep us stagnant. Comfort is not the enemy, complacency is. To be complacent enough to not seek the truth for yourself, leaving it buried beneath the throne of lies where you sit comfortably. To seek the truth may be a tough endeavor, but it does not reject what we are taught or we know so far. All that it asks is for us not to accept it unexamined, let belief step out the darkness of falsehoods, and hold it up to the light of truth. To be certain, in your wisdom, that what you know is right. \
\
As Socrates did not counsel us to know nothing, only that we do not yet know, and never will know everything. But to keep looking at the things with your own truth, think outside the herd, trends, and the cave we built ourselves with complacency. You only needs to ask, quietly and often: is this true because I have found it to be so, or true because I was simply told?\
\
Perhaps that is enough, not to abandon the world and the knowledge that made us who we are. But to finally meet it with our eyes candidly, our feet firm and tall, and choose for ourselves what we are certain of.
