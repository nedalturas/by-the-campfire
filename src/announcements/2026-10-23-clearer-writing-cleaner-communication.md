We're excited to introduce two powerful new updates designedto improve the writing quality and communication for our dear readers!

### What's New?

 

#### Proofreading feature

We want to give you a worthwhile read, so we make sure eachpost is polished before it reaches our audience. 

We have decided to implement a proofreading workflow feature;you will not be able to see it, as this is an internal tool that we use and haveintegrated into our content manager. How does it work?

We use a three-step workflow: Draft, In Review, and Ready

1. Our authors will create a draft in the Draftcolumn and will stay there until they’re done; once they’re done, they willneed to submit it to the In Review Column
2. Proofreaders will be notified when authors havesubmitted a draft for review, and they will start the proofreading process.After writing feedback, they will move it back to the Draft column so the authorswill be notified about the fix that needs their attention
3. The process above repeats in vice versa untilthe proofreader will find it good to go. The proofreader will move it to theReady column and publish the post; the author will also be prompted that hiswork has been published in our internal channel.

There will still be a delay with our posts because of thisnewly implemented feature, but we’d rather have a delayed quality post ratherthan a quick but unpolished one.

 

#### Announcement Board Feature

To keep everyone on the same page with what our developerand authors have been working on, we made a centralized page for that purpose.You can see a banner on the very top of our page which you can either click thelink to read the full details or dismiss it, you can also navigate to the very bottomand find relevant links as well as the announcement link that will redirect youto the announcement board.
