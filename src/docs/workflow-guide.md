## Overview

This guide explains how content moves from an initial idea to publication on By the Campfire.

### For Writers
1. Log in to the CMS at `/admin/`.
2. Create a new entry in **Literature**.
3. Fill in your Title, Date, Description, and Story body.
4. When finished drafting, set the status to **In Review**.

### For Proofreaders
1. Review entries in the **In Review** column.
2. Leave constructive notes in **Editor Notes**.
3. When approved, move the status to **Ready to Publish** and publish the piece.
